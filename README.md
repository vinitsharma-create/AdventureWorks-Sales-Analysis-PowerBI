# AdventureWorks Sales Analysis | Power BI

> An interactive Power BI project analyzing sales, products, customers, territories, and returns using data modeling, DAX, and business-focused visualizations.

---

## Project Overview

This project analyzes AdventureWorks sales data across multiple related tables to understand overall business performance, product profitability, customer behavior, sales trends, and return patterns.

The project transforms raw transactional data into an interactive Power BI dashboard using data modeling, Power Query, DAX measures, KPIs, and business-oriented visualizations.

---

## Business Objectives

- Analyze revenue, orders, profit, and product performance.
- Identify high-performing and high-return products and categories.
- Understand customer purchasing behavior and customer segments.
- Analyze sales and orders across territories.
- Compare weekday and weekend purchasing behavior.
- Track monthly and historical sales performance.
- Evaluate returned products and return rates.
- Identify high-value customers and profitable products.

## Key Business Insights

- **Bikes generated the highest revenue at approximately $23.6M**, making them the dominant revenue category.
- **Accessories contributed approximately 60% of total orders**, making them the highest-volume category.
- **Accessories recorded the highest return quantity with 1,130 units**, representing approximately **61.82% of total returned quantity**.
- Although Accessories had the highest absolute number of returned units, their return rate was approximately **1.955%** due to their high order volume.
- **Bikes had the highest category-level return rate at approximately 3.08%**, making them an important area for return-rate monitoring.
- The overall **return rate was approximately 2.172%**, with **1,828 returned units** and approximately **$765K in returned amount**.
- **Weekday orders (40,338)** were significantly higher than **weekend orders (15,708)**, indicating stronger weekday purchasing activity.
- Accessories generated approximately **21.2K bulk orders**, significantly higher than Clothing.
- Customer orders were almost evenly distributed by gender, with **Male customers at 50.43% and Female customers at 49.57%**.
- Customers in the **Average income category contributed approximately 67.35% of total orders**, making it the largest customer segment.
- **Jordan Turner** was identified as the highest-revenue customer, generating approximately **$15.57K in revenue**.
- **Water Bottle – 30 oz** was identified as the most ordered product.
- **Mountain-200 Black, 46** was identified as the most profitable product.

## Business Recommendations

- Prioritize high-performing bike products while closely monitoring their relatively higher return rate.
- Investigate products with unusually high return rates to identify potential quality or customer-experience issues.
- Maintain sufficient inventory for high-volume accessory products.
- Use customer-level revenue and purchasing behavior to identify and retain high-value customers.
- Explore strategies to increase weekend sales while maintaining strong weekday performance.
- Use product profitability, order volume, and return trends to support inventory and product portfolio decisions.
- Monitor category and product-level return rates instead of relying only on absolute return quantities.
